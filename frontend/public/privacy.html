Privacy Policy — Budjetame Last updated 2026-09-02

Budjetame is a personal finance app for the web and Android.

This policy explains what data Budjetame processes and how. By using Budjetame you agree to this policy.

Data controller

Budjetame is operated by its developer, Amedeo Pasanisi. For any privacy question or request, email amedeo.pasa@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data — when you register we store your email address and a salted password hash; when you sign in with Google we store the email address of your Google account. Your session token is valid for 90 days.
  • Financial data you enter — wallets, transactions (expenses, incomes, transfers), categories, recurring definitions, and import/export files. This data belongs to your account and is never shared with anyone.
  • Location data (optional) — a transaction may carry coordinates that you attach explicitly, with a "use my location" button or by picking a spot on the map. The coordinates are stored with that transaction for as long as the transaction exists. Location is never collected in the background.

How we use data

Only to provide the service: to store your data, show it back to you, derive balances and budget figures, and authenticate you. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking software.

Storage and security

Data is stored on servers in the European Union and transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as salted hashes; session tokens are kept encrypted on your device.

Deletion

You can delete your account and all its data at any time from the app (Settings → Delete account) or the web app's settings. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also ask us to delete your data by email.

Children

Budjetame is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published at this address with a new "last updated" date.
