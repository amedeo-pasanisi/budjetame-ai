# Daily Allowance is floored to the cent, remainder on the last day

The Budget's Daily Allowance divides Monthly Spendable by the days in the month and floors to the cent; the leftover remainder lands on the last day of the month, so the month still sums exactly to the Monthly Spendable. Exact rational math (16.666…/day, rounded only at display) was rejected because it broke the user's mental arithmetic: with 500€/April the allowance is 16.66€/day (500 ÷ 30 = 16.666…, floored to the cent), and spending three days' worth (49.98€ = 3 × 16.66) on day 1 leaves days 2 and 3 at exactly 0 — exact math would have left 0.02€ on day three. Floored per-day amounts keep every Spendable Today figure reproducible by hand, and the last-day remainder guarantees no cent is ever lost or invented.
